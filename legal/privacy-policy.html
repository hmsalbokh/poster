Privacy Policy — Poster (Scheduled Uploader)

Last updated: October 1, 2026

Poster ("the App") is a desktop application that schedules short videos stored in a local folder and publishes them to the user's own YouTube and TikTok accounts at scheduled times.

1. Data we collect

  • OAuth credentials you provide: Google OAuth client ID/secret and TikTok Client Key/secret that you paste into the App, plus the access and refresh tokens issued after you sign in. These are stored only on your own device, encrypted with the operating system's secure storage (Electron safeStorage).
  • Video metadata you provide: titles, descriptions, tags and scheduled publish times from the JSON sidecar files you place in the watched folder. These stay on your device except for the content you choose to publish.
  • Published content: when a scheduled time arrives, the App uploads the video file and its caption to YouTube and/or TikTok on your behalf and only to accounts you linked.

2. Data we do NOT collect

  • We operate no servers and run no analytics or tracking.
  • We never sell, share, or transmit your data to any third party except YouTube and TikTok APIs for the publishing actions you explicitly schedule.
  • We do not access your contacts, files outside the watched folder, or any other accounts.

3. TikTok data usage

The App requests the TikTok scopes user.info.basic, video.upload and video.publish solely to publish the videos you schedule, with the privacy level you set per video (default: private to you). Tokens are kept on your device and you can revoke access at any time from your TikTok settings or by unlinking in the App.

4. Data retention and deletion

All data lives on your device. Deleting the App's data folder or uninstalling the App removes stored tokens and settings. Published videos must be deleted from YouTube/TikTok directly. You can request help at the contact below.

5. Children's privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13 and collects no data from them.

6. Changes

Material changes to this policy will be reflected by updating the date above.
