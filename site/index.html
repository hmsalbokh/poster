Poster

Desktop app that schedules short videos from a local folder and auto-posts them to your social accounts.

View on GitHub

How it works

  1. Drop files in: place an .mp4 video plus a .json sidecar (title, description, tags, scheduled publish time, destinations) into the watched folder.
  2. Link accounts: connect your YouTube and TikTok accounts from the Account tab (OAuth, tokens stay encrypted on your device).
  3. Auto-publish: at the scheduled time Poster uploads each video to the selected destinations (YouTube, TikTok, or both) and tracks every attempt in the queue and log.

Features

  • Scheduled publishing to YouTube Shorts and TikTok (Direct Post).
  • Per-video destinations and TikTok privacy level, set in the sidecar file.
  • Live queue with statuses: pending, uploading, scheduled, published, failed — plus retry.
  • Automatic token refresh, encrypted local credential storage, no servers, no tracking.

Platforms

Windows desktop (Electron). Free and open source: github.com/hmsalbokh/poster
